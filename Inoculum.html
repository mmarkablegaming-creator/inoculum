Germicidal ultraviolet · 254 nm
Choose UV, then click to irradiate a 60 µm spot, or treat the whole field. One log of survivors costs about 2 mJ/cm² for E. coli and 12 for a spore. Survivors carry DNA damage: the SOS response raises their mutation rate and induces any prophage they carry.
